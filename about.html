About

I didn't plan a path into product — I worked every seat next to it first.

Sales, then customer success, then a stint building the tool myself — by the time I had the title "product manager," I'd already done most of the job without it.

I grew up in Nairobi, studied Computer Information Sciences at Stanford, and spent the next twelve years in the US — nine of them in Silicon Valley. I started at Tesla on the program management side of renewable energy rollouts, then joined Aurora Solar — first in sales, then customer success, where I ended up owning a $15M+ ARR portfolio across 700+ enterprise and mid-market accounts. Getting that close to what customers were actually struggling with is what pulled me into Product Data Ops, leading the build of a bespoke analytics platform that gave them real-time visibility into their own data. That was the first time I was building the thing instead of just representing it, and it's what made the move into product permanent.

I moved to London in 2025 and now work as an Associate Product Manager at Kavida.ai, building AI procurement and expediting agents for aerospace & defense clients. It's the most technically demanding product work I've done, and the closest I've felt to building the future rather than just reporting on it.
