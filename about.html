About

I got into product by way of the support queue.

Five-plus years into AI-driven SaaS, I still think the most useful thing a PM can do is spend real time with the people stuck using what you shipped.

I grew up in Nairobi, studied Computer Information Sciences at Stanford, and spent the next twelve years in the US — nine of them in Silicon Valley. I started at Tesla on the program management side of renewable energy rollouts, then spent five years at Aurora Solar, first managing a $5M+ enterprise portfolio and later owning the roadmap for Aurora Insights, the analytics platform that grew out of everything I'd learned from those accounts. That pattern — get close to the problem, then build the thing that solves it — is what pulled me from customer success into product for good.

I moved to London in 2025 and now work as an Associate Product Manager at Kavida.ai, building AI procurement and expediting agents for aerospace & defense clients. It's the most technically demanding product work I've done, and the closest I've felt to building the future rather than just reporting on it.
