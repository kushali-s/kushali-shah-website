01

Customer education & discovery

Discovery & content

Product & Strategic CS, Aurora Solar — Sep 2019–Jan 2023

Problem

Across 700+ SMB and enterprise accounts, the same points of confusion kept surfacing — but nothing was capturing them in a way product or engineering could act on.

Approach

Ran structured account reviews and interviews, wrote three help-centre articles targeting the most common friction points, and built out two full customer case studies from my own book of business.

Outcome

The patterns from this work became the qualitative case for Aurora Insights — the analytics platform below — and shaped its first roadmap.

02

Aurora Insights — customer analytics platform

0→1 platform build

Product Data Ops, Aurora Solar — Jan 2023–Feb 2024

Problem

Enterprise customers had no visibility into their own usage data, which meant Aurora had no reliable early signal for churn risk or expansion opportunity across a $15M+ portfolio.

Approach

Owned the roadmap end to end: mapped customer pain points to technical feasibility, secured buy-in across the C-suite, Legal and Engineering, then built SQL-based data pipelines and 15+ Looker dashboards, automating insights with Python models.

Outcome

Rolled out company-wide, training 50+ internal users and cutting manual reporting effort by 75%. Ran A/B tests on reporting layouts post-launch, tripling query volume and lifting engagement 70%.

03

AI procurement & expediting agents

0→1 AI agent features

Associate Product Manager, Kavida.ai — Aug 2025–present

Problem

Aerospace & defense procurement teams needed AI agents to automate supplier follow-up and reasoning — but agentic tools are a hard sell inside teams running high-stakes, zero-margin-for-error workflows.

Approach

Ran user research with 200+ operational stakeholders across three releases, writing PRDs and feature specs aligned to engineering constraints. Designed technical, semi-technical and non-technical UX flows, then built the "Magic Number" activation framework and engagement dashboards to track what adoption actually looked like — treating activation flow changes as testable hypotheses rather than one-off tweaks, the same instinct I'd bring to a formal A/B testing setup.

Outcome

Authored four onboarding guides and SOPs so the rollout could scale to new deployments without me in the room.
