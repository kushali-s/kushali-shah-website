Product Success Manager, Aurora Solar — Mar 2023–Feb 2024

Problem

Enterprise customers had no visibility into their own usage data, which meant Aurora had no reliable early signal for churn risk or expansion opportunity across a $5M+ portfolio.

Approach

Owned the roadmap end to end: mapped customer pain points to technical feasibility, secured buy-in across the C-suite, Legal and Engineering, then built SQL-based data pipelines and 15+ Looker dashboards, automating insights with Python models.

Outcome

Rolled out company-wide, training 50+ internal users and cutting manual reporting effort by 75% along the way.

Associate Product Manager, Kavida.ai — Jul 2025–present

Problem

Aerospace & defense procurement teams needed AI agents to automate supplier follow-up and reasoning — but agentic tools are a hard sell inside teams running high-stakes, zero-margin-for-error workflows.

Approach

Ran user research with 200+ operational stakeholders across three releases, converting it into PRDs aligned to engineering constraints. Designed technical, semi-technical and non-technical UX flows, then built the "Magic Number" activation framework and engagement dashboards to track what adoption actually looked like.

Outcome

Authored four onboarding guides and SOPs so the rollout could scale to new deployments without me in the room.
